# Adopters

Organizations that **currently and actively run Norviq** in an environment that matters to them —
production, staging with real agents, or a security evaluation that reached a decision.

That is CNCF's definition of an adopter, and it is the one used here. Stars, forks, blog mentions and
"we tried it once" do not qualify. An honest empty list is worth more than a padded one.

_There are no listed adopters yet._

## Adding yourself

Open a pull request adding a row. Say what you run it in front of and, if you can, what it caught or
what it cost you. A contact who can confirm the entry is helpful; it is not required.
